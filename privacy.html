Privacy Policy

Who we are. Best Quote Tool is an online estimating tool for contractors and other trades, operated by FG BestWay Inc. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the tool handles and what it never touches.

Version 1.0 — Last updated November 17, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address. You sign up with your email and sign in with a one-time code we email you. Our account system (provided by Supabase) stores your email so your account works.
  • Your subscription status. Whether you are on the free plan or a paid subscription, how many of your free tests you have used, and your Cloud Saving on/off setting, if you have that add-on.
  • Your acceptance and security records. Which version of the Terms of Service you agreed to and when, and sign-in security records such as the office-access sessions you create — kept so we can prove the agreement you accepted and keep your account secure.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by email or phone for support, we keep that conversation so we can answer you.
  • That is all. We do not ask for or store your rates, your quotes, or your customers' information on the base plan (see below).

How we use your information

We use the information above only to run your account, sign you in, unlock the features your plan includes, process your subscription through Stripe, send you sign-in codes and service notices (including notices about changes to the Terms or this policy), answer your support requests, and meet our legal obligations. We do not use it for anything else.

What we never collect on the base plan

Your saved quotes, the rates you enter, and any client details you type into an estimate stay on your own device (in your browser or in the app). They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them. We never see your rates or your client data. If you clear your browser data or change devices, those on-device copies are gone — the document you download or print is your permanent copy.

Cloud Saving (only if you switch it on)

Cloud Saving is an optional paid add-on. If — and only if — you subscribe to it and switch it on, the documents you generate are also stored in your account so you can open them on another device. That stored copy can include the estimate details you generated. You can delete any cloud-stored document at any time from your account panel, and switching Cloud Saving off stops any further storage. If you never switch it on, this section never applies to you.

Payments

Paid subscriptions are handled by Stripe, our payment processor. Stripe collects and processes your card details on its own secure pages. We never see, receive, or store your card number. Stripe sends us only the result — whether your subscription is active — so the tool knows which features to unlock.

Refunds are covered in Clause 6 of the Terms of Service: a full refund of the first month is available within 7 days of your first subscription payment, once per account; after the first month, payments are not refundable, and you may cancel at any time.

Cookies and local storage

The tool uses your browser's local storage to keep your saved quotes and settings on your device, and to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use tracking cookies to follow you around the web.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, sized to our business, to protect the information we hold: sign-in is by one-time email code rather than a password, account and cloud data are held by our provider (Supabase) behind access controls, and information sent between your device and our providers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach involving your personal information happens, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

What we do not do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not read, use, or sell your quotes, rates, or client data — on the base plan we never receive them at all.

The only sharing that happens is functional: your account information is handled by the providers that run the service — Supabase (accounts and, for Cloud Saving users, document storage) and Stripe (payments) — only so they can provide those functions. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your account information (email and subscription status) is kept while your account is active. Records of which version of the Terms you agreed to, and when, are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward, as our contract record. You may ask us to delete your account and the information tied to it at any time by contacting us at the address below; cloud-stored documents, if any, are deleted with it, and we complete deletion within a reasonable time after your request — except for records we are required to keep for legal or tax reasons (payment records are held by Stripe under its own policy). Documents stored only on your own device are yours to delete whenever you like — we never had a copy.

Your choices about your information

You may ask us at any time, using the contact below, to tell you what account information we hold about you, to correct your email address or other account information, or to delete your account as described above. We will answer within a reasonable time.

Children

Best Quote Tool is a business tool for contractors and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will notify you by email and inside the tool at least 15 days before it takes effect and, where the change requires it, ask you to agree again before continuing — the same process described in Section 9 of the Terms of Service.
